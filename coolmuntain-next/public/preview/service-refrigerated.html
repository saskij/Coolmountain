The Service

Built Around
Your Cargo

Cool Mountain Transport specializes in the transport of temperature-sensitive freight. Whether you are shipping fresh produce, frozen goods, or pharmaceuticals, our state-of-the-art reefer units maintain precise temperatures from −20°F to +70°F throughout the entire journey.

Every load is dispatched by a team that answers the phone at any hour, on equipment that is late-model, inspected, and maintained on a strict preventative schedule.

What’s Included
  • Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) compliant cold chain
  • Remote temperature monitoring with downloadable logs
  • 24/7 dispatch support for time-sensitive deliveries
  • Pre-cooled and inspected before every load
  • Continuous set-point control from −20°F to +70°F

How It Runs

From Booking
to Delivery

Four steps, the same every time — so you always know where your freight is and who to call.

01

Pre-Cool & Inspect
