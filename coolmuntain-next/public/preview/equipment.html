The Fleet

Late-Model Tractors.
Serious Maintenance.

Cool Mountain Transport operates one of the newest fleets in the industry. Our tractors run under a strict preventative maintenance schedule, and our trailers are inspected before every load — because equipment that breaks down is freight that arrives late.

Every unit carries collision-avoidance technology, tire-pressure monitoring with temperature readings, and telematics that give your dispatcher and ours the same picture at the same time.

Safety & Technology

A Second Set of Eyes
in Every Tractor

Active safety systems are standard across the fleet — not an upgrade.

01 · COLLISION

Collision Avoidance

Meritor/Wabco and Bendix active safety systems with automatic emergency braking.

02 · EVIDENCE

Collision Cameras

Road-facing cameras on every unit — objective documentation for every mile.

03 · TRACKING

Lane Departure Warning

Alerts the driver the moment a tractor drifts outside its lane.

04 · BLIND SPOT

Side Radar

Continuous side-object detection covering the length of the trailer.

05 · FATIGUE

Drowsy Driving Detection

In-cab monitoring that flags fatigue before it becomes an incident.

06 · MAINTENANCE

Tire Pressure & Temp

In-cab tire pressure warning system with live temperature readings.
