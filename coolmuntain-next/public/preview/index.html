The Company

Strategic Logistics.
Uncompromising Reliability.

Cool Mountain Transport is more than a carrier — we’re your strategic partner in the heart of the Pacific Northwest.

Based in Nampa, Idaho, we combine the reach of a national logistics provider with the personalized service of a dedicated local team. Our success is built on a safety-first culture, an elite fleet of late-model equipment, and professional drivers who treat every load with the care it deserves — moving your business forward on time, every time.

To deliver outstanding transportation services, provide exceptional customer service, improve technology, and create business efficiencies that promote long-term customer stability, longevity and prosperity.
