The Company

Strategic Logistics.
Uncompromising Reliability.

Since its inception, Cool Mountain Transport has focused on safe, sensible growth, founded on financial stability and customer collaboration to achieve the highest transportation standards.

Based in Nampa, Idaho, we combine the reach of a national logistics provider with the personalised service of a dedicated local team. We maintain an attentive management style that maximises organisational efficiency, improves operations, reduces costs and implements safety standards.

Each load is handled with great care in order to deliver every customer’s freight safely, on time and per specifications — whether that is a single dry van across the state or a temperature-controlled run across the country.

Our Mission

To deliver outstanding transportation services, provide exceptional customer service, improve technology, and create business efficiencies that promote long-term customer stability, longevity and prosperity.
